Engineered for Campus Efficiency

Clean architectural separation with Express REST APIs, MongoDB Mongoose models, and modern Vanilla JavaScript.

Role-Based JWT Auth

Dual role separation for Administrators and Students. Salted bcrypt password hashing and 30-day JWT bearer tokens.

7-Day Dynamic Menu

Covers all 28 weekly meal slots (Breakfast, Lunch, Snacks, Dinner) with timing schedules, calories, and dietary filters.

Fee & Payment Tracking

Track monthly mess subscriptions, generate unique transaction IDs, filter Paid/Pending status, and print digital receipts.

Live Meal Check-Ins

Students record real-time meal consumption with one-click attendance check-ins, feeding live kitchen headcount analytics.

Official Notices

Admin broadcast center for urgent fee deadlines, festival feasts, and routine sanitation closures with priority badges.

Student Feedback Loop

Transparent rating system with category-wise reviews (Food Quality, Hygiene, Timing) and official warden responses.
